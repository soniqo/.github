**[Meet Mimi →](https://soniqo.audio/robot)** — A small robot with planned local voice control through a nearby laptop or phone. Assembly and voice-control development are in progress.

### Open-source SDKs

**[speech-swift](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-swift)** — Speech recognition, synthesis, speech-to-speech, VAD, diarization and audio processing for Apple Silicon, using MLX and CoreML.

**[speech-android](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android)** — Kotlin SDK and Android integration for local speech recognition, synthesis, VAD and audio processing.

**[speech-core](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-core)** — C++ speech runtime and voice-agent pipelines, with ONNX Runtime and LiteRT backends.

**Model downloads:** [Apple models](https://huggingface.co/aufklarer) · [ONNX and LiteRT models](https://huggingface.co/soniqo)

### ML Engineering Services

We create datasets, train and adapt models, and integrate them into your product. Work starts with your target tasks, data, languages and hardware.

- **Custom datasets** — Collect, curate and annotate data for your task, languages and domain, with quality checks and separate training and evaluation sets.
- **Model training and adaptation** — Adapt speech, audio and search models to your users, vocabulary and workflows.
- **Evaluation and benchmarking** — Measure accuracy, quality, latency and memory on representative data and target hardware.
- **Inference and deployment** — Convert and optimize models, integrate runtimes and SDKs, and deploy on devices or private infrastructure.
- **Audio and search engineering** — Speech enhancement, spatial audio, retrieval, ranking and research workflows.
